Pips & Peril

A retro dice roguelike for Android, Windows and Steam Deck. Roll, hold and reroll your way past three kings.

Download the game See how it plays

One turn, five moves.

  1. Pick a stance: attack, brace or focus.
  2. Roll your damage dice and the D4 modifier.
  3. Hold the dice you like and reroll the rest.
  4. Tap a target. Pairs and triples add damage and coins.
  5. Defend on their turn. Every pip blocks 10%, and anything over 100% is reflected back.

Try a roll.

Two d6 and the D4 modifier. Tap a die to hold it, then reroll once. A pair adds 3 before the modifier.

Three stances before every roll.

Attack
A normal hit.
Brace
Your hit is 30% weaker and can’t crit, but your next defence roll gets one extra pip.
Focus
Skip the attack and bank a reroll for later. You can hold two, and they carry into later fights.

Three castles, three kings.

The Gold King’s coins

  • Up to three living coins a round.
  • Each turn a coin attacks or flips.
  • Heads hits harder; tails blocks more of your damage.

The King of Cards

  • A card’s value, ace to king, sets its health and attack.
  • Any two cards can stack into one bigger card.
  • The king summons low cards to help.

The Jack King

  • One enemy that switches between two stances.
  • Bounce blocks your hit; grab hits back.
  • Roll two crits to knock its jacks loose.

Between fights.

The shop
Opens after rounds 2, 4 and 6. It sells special dice, extra d6s, modifier and reroll upgrades, and a full heal. You start with two damage dice and can grow to four.
The jam
Every purchase except the heal strains the machine. The first is safe, then the jam risk is 25%, 50% and 75%. A jam eats your coins and throws you out of the shop.
Ascension
Each hero has five harder levels. Win a full run at one level to open the next for that hero.
Profiles
Up to five players can share one install, each with their own unlocks, records and saved run.

Made in Godot, scored in chiptune.

Engine
Godot 4.3, with a balance simulator that plays hundreds of runs to test every change.
Art
Generated with ElevenLabs image models, then cut down to a 16-bit palette with custom Python tools.
Sound
Original chiptune music and effects, synthesised by a script written for the game.
Controls
Touch, a controller, or mouse and keyboard.
