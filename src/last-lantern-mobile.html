    How a fight works

    The light is the battlefield.

    1. Inside the circle, you can strike a shadow and see what it will do. Outside it, you see only eyes.
    2. Lamp light strips the Veil (the blue squares). A bared shadow loses its next turn.
    3. Set the lantern down and the blade cuts a bared shadow for 2. A kill gives you another action.
    4. Every spend draws the circle in, and each round burns 1 Flame.
    5. At 0 Flame the lamp smolders. Defend the ember and it relights.
    Attack
    3 Flame, or free with the blade
    Flare
    12 Flame. Bares every shadow in a cone.
    Burst
    25 Flame and 5 Wick. Bares every shadow.
    Defend
    Blows cost half and no Wick. With the lantern down, it becomes Wait.
