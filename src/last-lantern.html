I · The light

Light is the only thing you have.

Disasters drove people underground. They built cities and grew forests in the dark. You play a scholar, beaten and robbed of his scrolls, who wakes in the dungeons at the very bottom.

Flame
How much light the lantern holds, and how far it reaches. It burns down as you go. Every action spends it, and you can never spend the last of it.
Wick
The lantern’s wear. Heavy blows and big spells char it, and the Flame can never be higher than the Wick.
Smolder
At 0 Flame the lamp gutters to an ember. Protect it and it relights. One more blow and it goes out.

II · The battle

The light is the battlefield.

  1. A fight starts where you stand when a shadow sees the lamp. You don’t go to a different screen.
  2. Inside the circle, a shadow can be struck, and you can see its Veil and what it will do next. Outside it, you see only eyes.
  3. Lamp light strips the Veil. A bared shadow loses its next turn and can be cut by the blade.
  4. Every spend draws the circle in. Each Flare or strike makes the lit area smaller.
  5. Each round burns 1 Flame. You go first, then each shadow, nearest first.

IV · The commands

Every command costs light.

The menu has Attack, Flame, Defend and Items. Flame opens the lantern skills. Items will hold the things you carry, and they are still being designed.

CommandFlameWhat it does
Attack (lamp)3Strips 1 Veil from a lit shadow. A bared shadow takes 1 damage.
Attack (blade)0With the lantern set down: 2 damage to a bared shadow. A kill gives you one more action.
Flare12Bares every shadow in a cone, lit or not.
Burst25Bares every shadow around you. Also chars 5 Wick.
Set down or lift0Once a turn, and it doesn’t use your turn. Blows cost half again as much Flame while the lantern is down.
Defend0Until your next turn, blows cost half the Flame and no Wick. With the lantern set down, it becomes Wait and only passes the turn.

A Creeper’s blow costs 10 Flame and 2 Wick. The pack lands at most two blows a round. All numbers are from the prototype and will change with playtesting.

V · The making

Made in Godot, lit for real.

  • Godot 4.7 and GDScript. I’m making it on my own, in my spare time.
  • HD-2D. Pixel-art figures stand in a 3D temple. The camera looks down at an angle with a long lens.
  • Real light. The lamp is a point light, and walls block it, so rooms you can’t see into stay black.
  • Sprites made with code. A Python pipeline builds every frame of the cast into one sprite sheet, with a separate sheet for the parts that glow, like eyes and flames.
  • Progress that carries over. Each fight ends with a summary of the Flame and Wick you spent and the XP you earned. XP will go into a skill tree that carries over between runs.
